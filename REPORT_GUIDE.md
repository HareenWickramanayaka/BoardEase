# Report checklist

Use the separate `BoardEase_Mini_Project_Report_Template.docx` file. The document is **six pages** before inserting screenshots, so you have up to four more pages of room (maximum 10 pages).

1. On the cover page replace the name and index placeholders.
2. Take genuine screenshots from the application you run locally. Replace the six screenshot placeholders on pages 4 and 5. Resize them to fit.
3. Complete the "Actual result / status" column on the testing page based on what you really tested.
4. Check that the claims about authentication, listing management and validation match the version you submitted.
5. Update the submission date as needed, proofread, and remove the orange action-required notes.
6. Export to PDF and ensure total pages <= 10.
7. Name the final report and demo video using your own index number.

The separate `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` contains a camera-on video outline under 5 minutes.
